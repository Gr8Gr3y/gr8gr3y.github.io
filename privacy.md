## Part II — Privacy Policy (Google Play Data Safety Aligned)

### 9. Local-First Privacy Architecture
AeBRen operates on a strict **Local-First** principle: your e-book files, directory trees, annotations, covers, filenames, and selected PDF text/OCR fragments are processed **locally on your device**. The Developer does not operate user accounts, does not collect personal library inventories, and does not upload your books to Developer servers.

### 10. Permissions & On-Device Storage
- **Storage Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Android SAF):** Used exclusively upon user action to scan folders, read book metadata, rename/move files in-place, embed metadata/covers, and save user-initiated exports.
- **Local Preferences & Emergency Recovery:** Templates, Regex rules, favorite folders, UI theme/language, and signed Free daily usage counters remain stored locally on your device. Only in the event of a fatal startup crash that prevents the UI from initializing does the Software write a local recovery snapshot (`AeBRen_emergency_settings_backup.json`) to your Downloads folder.
- **Local App Integrity & Anti-Tampering Checks:** To protect package integrity and prevent unauthorized modification or repackaging, AeBRen performs strictly local, on-device runtime integrity and digital signature verification within its own application process. No system telemetry, memory maps, process data, or diagnostic reports from these checks are collected, stored externally, or transmitted off your device.

### 11. Internet Access & Third-Party Services
- **User-Initiated Cover & Catalog Search (Pro):** Cover search opens standard search links in your device’s external browser without scraping or downloading third-party cover images inside the app; only image files explicitly selected by you from your device are embedded locally. When you trigger Bibliographic Lookup in the metadata helper, only your search query terms (title, author, ISBN, DOI, or short bibliographic citation) are sent to public bibliographic APIs (`Open Library`, `Crossref`, `Wikidata` — plus optional `Internet Archive`, `Library of Congress SRU`, `Deutsche Nationalbibliothek SRU`, `OpenAlex`, or custom `SRU`/`OAI-PMH` gateways when connected to a compatible server proxy), subject to their privacy policies. When triggering fragment OCR for a language not yet cached on your device, `Tesseract.js` may download the corresponding OCR language pack on demand.
- **Advertising (Free Tier Only):** In the Free tier, `com.aebren.app` integrates the **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK**, which may process Advertising ID, approximate IP-based location, diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics under Google’s Privacy Policy and regional consent settings (GDPR/UMP). When Pro access is unlocked via Google Play Billing, ad display and ad requests are disabled.

### 12. Data Retention, Children & Contact
Because no central user databases exist, clearing app data or uninstalling AeBRen erases all local preferences and counters (except user-exported files in Downloads). The Software is not directed to children under 13 (or applicable age of digital consent).
