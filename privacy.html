Part II · Privacy Policy (Google Play Data Safety Aligned)

9. Local-First Privacy Architecture

Serhiy Yuzchyshyn (#Gr3y) respects your privacy. AeBRen operates on a strict Local-First principle: your e-book files, directory trees, annotations, covers, filenames, and selected PDF text or OCR fragments are processed locally on your device.

The Developer does not operate user accounts, does not require registration, does not collect personal library inventories, and does not upload, harvest, sell, or store your books on Developer-controlled servers.

10. Permissions & On-Device Storage

  • Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Android SAF): Used exclusively upon user action to browse folders, read book metadata headers, rename or move files in-place, embed metadata and covers into books, and save user-initiated exports.
  • Local Preferences & Emergency Recovery: Templates, Regex rules, favorite folders, UI theme, language, and signed Free daily usage counters remain stored locally on your device. Only in the event of a fatal startup crash that prevents the UI from initializing does the Software write a local recovery snapshot (AeBRen_emergency_settings_backup.json) to your Downloads folder.
  • Local App Integrity & Anti-Tampering Checks: To protect package integrity and prevent unauthorized modification or repackaging, AeBRen performs strictly local, on-device runtime integrity and digital signature verification within its own application process. No system telemetry, memory maps, process data, or diagnostic reports from these checks are collected, stored externally, or transmitted off your device.

11. Internet Access & Third-Party Services

The Software requests the INTERNET permission solely for the following transparent purposes:

  • User-Initiated Cover & Catalog Search (Pro): Cover search opens standard search links in your device's external browser without scraping or downloading third-party cover images inside the app; only image files explicitly selected by you from your device are embedded locally. When you explicitly trigger Bibliographic Lookup in the metadata helper, only your search query terms (title, author, ISBN, DOI, or short bibliographic citation) are sent to public bibliographic APIs (Open Library, Crossref, Wikidata — plus optional Internet Archive, Library of Congress SRU, Deutsche Nationalbibliothek SRU, OpenAlex, or custom SRU/OAI-PMH gateways when connected to a compatible server proxy), subject to their privacy policies. When triggering fragment OCR for a language not yet cached locally, Tesseract.js may download the corresponding OCR language pack on demand.
  • Advertising in Free Tier Only (Google AdMob & UMP): In the Free tier, com.aebren.app integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK (play-services-ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to display banner and interstitial ads and manage regional consent (GDPR/CCPA). Google may automatically collect and process device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), approximate IP-based location, diagnostics, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and your consent settings. When Pro access is unlocked via Google Play Billing, ad display and ad requests are disabled.
